## 6. How the Process Works

*Component Note: Step-by-step project progression.*

**Section Tag:** How It Works

**Section Title (H2):** From Layout to First Dinner

**Section Description:** We order the cabinets early so the build doesn’t stall waiting on them.

### Step 01: Design Visit

We measure the room and talk through how you use it. You tell us what drives you crazy about the current kitchen — that’s usually the most useful part.

### Step 02: Plan & Quote

A layout and a written price. Once you approve it, cabinets and counters get ordered so they arrive when we need them, not three weeks after.

### Step 03: Build

Demo, then rough-in, then cabinets, then counters, then backsplash. We set up a temporary spot for your kettle and microwave so you’re not completely stuck.

### Step 04: Handover

Appliances connected, drawers adjusted, everything cleaned, and a walkthrough with you before we call it finished.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

*Component Note: The questions homeowners ask most before booking a kitchen renovation.*

**Section Tag:** Questions

**Section Title (H2):** Frequently Asked Questions

**Section Description:** The things people ask us most before booking a kitchen renovation.

### Q1: How long does a kitchen renovation take?

**Answer:** Four to six weeks for most full kitchens, once the cabinets are on site. The counter template has to happen after the cabinets are set, and that adds about a week in the middle.

### Q2: Will I be without a kitchen the whole time?

**Answer:** You’ll be without the sink and stove for most of it. We set up a temporary counter with your kettle, microwave, and fridge so you’ve got something to work with.

### Q3: Can you remove the wall between the kitchen and living room?

**Answer:** Usually. If it’s load-bearing we bring in a beam and do it properly with the right permits. We’ll tell you at the design visit which kind of wall you have.

### Q4: Do you do custom cabinets or stock?

**Answer:** Both. Stock is quicker and cheaper and fine for a lot of layouts. Custom makes sense when the room is an odd shape or you want the storage designed around specific things.

### Q5: Can I keep my existing appliances?

**Answer:** Yes — just tell us early so we build the openings to fit them. If you’re replacing them later, get the model numbers to us and we’ll size for the new ones now.
