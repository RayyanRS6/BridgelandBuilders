## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

*Component Note: The questions homeowners ask most before booking a whole home renovation.*

**Section Tag:** Questions

**Section Title (H2):** Frequently Asked Questions

**Section Description:** The things people ask us most before booking a whole home renovation.

### Q1: How long does a whole home renovation take?

**Answer:** Most of ours run eight to sixteen weeks depending on the size of the house and how much of it we’re touching. You get a schedule with your quote, and if something shifts we tell you the same week — not at the end.

### Q2: Can we stay in the house while you work?

**Answer:** Often yes, if we stage it in sections and keep a kitchen and bathroom live for you. Sometimes it’s faster and cheaper to move out for a few weeks. We’ll lay out both options at the walkthrough and let you decide.

### Q3: Do you handle permits?

**Answer:** Yes. Where the work needs a permit from the City of Winnipeg, we pull it and we schedule the inspections. You don’t have to chase any of that.

### Q4: Do I pick the materials or do you?

**Answer:** You do — we just narrow it down. We’ll bring options that fit your budget and hold up long term, and you choose from there. If you’d rather hand it to us entirely, that’s fine too.

### Q5: What happens if you find something unexpected behind a wall?

**Answer:** It happens on older homes — old wiring, water damage, sometimes a wall that’s holding more than it should. We stop, show you what we found, and price the fix before we carry on. No surprise line items at the end.
