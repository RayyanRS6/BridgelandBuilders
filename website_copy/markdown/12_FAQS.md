## 2. General Frequently Asked Questions

*Component Note: Core questions covering project types, estimates, permits, and living on site.*

### Q1: What types of renovation projects do you take on?

**Answer:** We handle whole-home, kitchen, bathroom, basement, and commercial renovations across Winnipeg, along with selected specialty construction work.

### Q2: Do you provide a written quote?

**Answer:** Yes. After we understand the space and scope, we provide a clear written quote so you know what is included before work begins.

### Q3: Do you handle permits and inspections?

**Answer:** Yes, when a project requires permits or inspections we include them in the plan and coordinate the required steps with the City of Winnipeg.

### Q4: Can I stay in my home during the renovation?

**Answer:** Often, yes. It depends on the size and location of the work. We explain what will be usable, how dust will be controlled, and when temporary disruptions are expected.

### Q5: How do I get started?

**Answer:** Use the quote form or call 431-866-5644. We will ask a few questions about your project and arrange the right next step, usually a free site visit.
