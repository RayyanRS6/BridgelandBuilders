## 3. Why Choose Bridgeland for Bathroom Renovation

*Component Note: Detailed pitch and philosophy.*

**Section Tag:** Why Bridgeland for your bath?

**Section Title (H2):** The part that matters is the part you can’t see

**Floating Badge:** Waterproofed Properly: Membrane and sloped base before a single tile goes down.

### Paragraph 1

A bathroom is a small room that punishes shortcuts. Water gets into anything you skip. Most of the failed bathrooms we get called to fix look fine on the surface — the problem started behind the tile two years earlier.

### Paragraph 2

So we do the boring parts properly. Backer board instead of drywall in the wet areas, a proper waterproofing membrane, a shower base sloped the way it should be, and the plumbing checked before anything gets closed up. Then we tile.

### Paragraph 3

Bathrooms are also the job people live around, so we keep it tight. We protect the hallway, we clean up daily, and we don’t leave the room torn open for a month. Most Winnipeg bathrooms we do are finished in two to three weeks from demo to the last bead of silicone.

## 6. How the Process Works

*Component Note: Step-by-step project progression.*

**Section Tag:** How It Works

**Section Title (H2):** Four Steps to a Finished Bathroom

**Section Description:** Short project, tight schedule, and you know the price before we start.

### Step 01: Measure

We come out, measure the room, look at the plumbing you’ve already got, and talk about what you want to change. Takes about half an hour.

### Step 02: Fixed Quote

You get one written price covering labour, materials, and haul-away. If you want to swap a tile or a vanity, we re-price it before anyone orders anything.

### Step 03: Build

Demo, rough-in, waterproofing, tile, then fixtures. Hallways get protected, the room gets sealed off, and we sweep up every day.

### Step 04: Final Check

We run the water, check every seal, and walk it with you. Anything you’re not happy with gets fixed before we invoice.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

*Component Note: The questions homeowners ask most before booking a bathroom renovation.*

**Section Tag:** Questions

**Section Title (H2):** Frequently Asked Questions

**Section Description:** The things people ask us most before booking a bathroom renovation.

### Q1: How long will my bathroom be out of service?

**Answer:** Two to three weeks for a typical full bath. If it’s your only bathroom, tell us at the measure — we plan those tighter and get the toilet back in service as early as we can.

### Q2: Can you move the toilet or shower to a different spot?

**Answer:** Usually yes. It depends on where the drains run, especially on a concrete slab. We’ll tell you at the measure whether it’s a small change or a bigger one, and what it adds.

### Q3: Do you supply the tile and fixtures or do I?

**Answer:** Either way works. Most people pick from options we bring, some prefer to shop themselves. If you supply it, just have it on site before we start so the schedule holds.

### Q4: Do you install heated floors?

**Answer:** Yes, and a bathroom reno is the right time to do it since the floor is already open. It’s a small add while we’re in there and a big difference in a Winnipeg winter.

### Q5: What if you find water damage under the old shower?

**Answer:** We find it fairly often. We photograph it, show you, and price the repair before we go further. Rotten subfloor gets replaced — we won’t tile over it.
